# NorthList Posts kit

Instructions that let your AI app write social posts for you and send them to [NorthList Posts](https://posts.northlist.site). You read them there, edit them, and post them to X and LinkedIn with one tap. Nothing is posted without you.

Your AI does the work with its own web search and your own AI plan. The app only shows the drafts and posts the ones you pick.

## What it writes

Each day, in the mix your goal asks for:
- **What you shipped**: from your own code history (commit messages and changed file names, never secrets or code).
- **News with your take**: fresh articles on your interests, read in full, always with the source link.

Your product is mentioned in about 1 in 3 posts at most, only where it fits.

## Works in

Claude Code (also the Code tab of the Claude desktop app), Codex and Antigravity. "What you shipped" needs an app that can read folders on your computer, which all three can.

## Start

1. Get this folder: download it from GitHub (Code › Download ZIP) or clone it.
2. Open it in your AI app.
3. In NorthList Posts, open **Connect**, create a setup prompt and paste it into the AI app in this folder. It connects itself and asks a few setup questions.
4. Each morning, type `daily`. The drafts show up on **Today** in a few minutes.

Other commands: `write about <anything>`, `setup`, `rewrite <title>: <what to change>`, `connect`.

## Privacy

- This folder never stores personal data or your token. The token lives only in your AI app's own settings.
- Your AI reads your code history only from the folders you name, and never puts private details in a post.
- The app gets only what your AI sends: your profile answers and the drafts.
